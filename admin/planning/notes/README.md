## 🎯 When to Create Notes

Create planning notes for:
- Early-stage exploration and brainstorming
- Comparing multiple approaches before deciding
- Documenting decision-making processes
- Capturing ideas that may not be immediately actionable
- Exploratory research and feasibility studies

## 🔄 When to Archive Notes

Archive notes when:
- The feature/idea has been implemented
- The content has been consolidated into a feature plan
- The approach has been decided and documented elsewhere
- The note is no longer referenced or relevant
- The planning phase has transitioned to implementation

**Note:** Archived notes are kept for historical context and to understand the decision-making process.
